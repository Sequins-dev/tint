# Untyped Octane M3 · Flat general arrays, compact OrdinaryObject, polymorphic store IC, and dominating property CSE · Darwin arm64

Milestone 3 of the untyped Octane optimization stream on top of `main` (`778cd18eb`): flat contiguous general-element array storage (`generalCount`, `generalTags: Uint8Array`, `generalRefs` via native `ReferenceVector`, lazy `numbers: Float64Array`, eliminating per-element `ElementCell` boxing), four new IR instructions (`general-element-load`, `general-element-store`, `general-slot-load`, `general-slot-store`, packed IR opcodes `125`–`128`) with AArch64 (extracted into `packages/codegen/src/aarch64/scalar/general-elements.ts`) and x86-64 emitters plus fast steady-state general-array `scriptArrayPushFast`, compact `OrdinaryObject` layout (`280 B → 248 B` by packing four boolean flags into `internalFlags` and moving `internalExecutionContext` to `OrdinaryRareSlots`), 4-entry polymorphic `property-store` inline caches with stale-transition epoch reuse against `propertyEpoch.value`, same-receiver dominating `property-load` CSE (`forwardDominatingProperties` in `packages/ir/src/optimization/property-cache.ts`), script-gated `general-is` folding (`packages/ir/src/optimization/forward-loads.ts`), type-narrowed `==`/`!=`, arity-bucketed method candidate lookup, `maxInlineDepth: 4`, and `allocatorInlines < 48` (gated on `fn.scriptEntry === true`). Base `778cd18eb`; head `16d16d1f3`. Apple M5 Max, Node 26.13.1.

On Octane (18 paired rounds, 18/18 ≥75% host idle, 88.3% base / 87.8% M3 median idle), **all three OOP and allocation-heavy programs improve with tight, non-overlapping IQRs**: **`DeltaBlue` improves −19.59%** (`1.242 → 1.000 ms/op`, `15.8×` Node, down from `19.7×`), **`RayTrace` improves −7.51%** (`3.805 → 3.519 ms/op`, `7.1×` Node, down from `7.7×`), **`Richards` improves −3.88%** (`0.628 → 0.604 ms/op`, `13.4×` Node, down from `14.0×`), and **`NavierStokes` stays flat** (`2.720 → 2.714 ms/op`, `−0.30%`, `1.5×` Node). On the generality suite (18 paired rounds, 18/18 ≥75% idle), **5 of 18 kernels improve with IQRs excluding zero and zero kernels regress**: **`objectArray` drops −21.64%** (`6.449 → 5.047 ns`, `2.4×` Node), **`receivers2` drops −7.04%** (`12.960 → 12.099 ns`, `2.8×` Node), **`receivers4` drops −4.96%** (`13.711 → 12.932 ns`, `2.9×` Node), **`receivers8` drops −2.90%** (`26.338 → 25.717 ns`, `4.7×` Node), and **`arguments6` drops −2.38%** (`4.110 → 3.971 ns`, `1.7×` Node). On elements/builtins/creation (18 paired rounds, 18/18 ≥75% idle), **`objectElementRead` drops −27.13%** (`5.820 → 4.166 ns`, `2.5×` Node) and `objectArrayLiteral` shifts `−13.68%` (`41.963 → 36.454 ns`, `9.7×` Node, IQR spans zero), while `numberElementUpdate` rises `+23.05%` (`+0.217 ns`, still `0.7×` Node) and `pushLength` rises `+16.32%` (`+1.767 ns`, `3.2×` Node). Call and loop kernels (20 paired rounds, 20/20 ≥75% idle) remain well ahead of Node across all eight benchmarks (`0.05×`–`0.5×` Node). `string-bench --suite general` (18 paired rounds, 18/18 ≥75% idle) stays flat within noise (`upperCase` `−4.01%`, `json` `+0.83%`, both IQRs span zero). Typed AOT outputs — **frozen v7 `--runtime-tree`** and **`kernels` typed** — produce **bit-for-bit identical** `__TEXT,__text`, `__TEXT,__eh_frame`, `__TEXT,__unwind_info`, and `__DATA,__data` Mach-O sections versus `main` (`778cd18eb`). Both Darwin arm64 `stage1 == stage2` and native `stage1 --target linux-x86_64` cross-emission reproduce their stage-1 images byte for byte under LLDB.

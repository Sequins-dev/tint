# Untyped Octane M4 · Direct general slot targeting, 2-level property CSE, and AArch64 IC/barrier fixes · Darwin arm64

Milestone 4 of the untyped Octane optimization stream on top of M3 (`1d1256a87`): direct-to-slot `general` stack targeting (`planDirectGeneralTargets` in `packages/codegen/src/aarch64/scalar/general-elements.ts`) for single-use and fused `property-load`, `general-element-load`, `general-slot-load`, `general-wrap`, `general-none`, `general-null`, `call`, and `call-indirect` stores plus pipelined `copyGeneralPair` for offsets `> 504` and paired `general` slot loads (all gated on `hasScriptOps` to keep typed AOT bit-identical); AArch64 IC and write-barrier fixes (replacing `±32 KiB` `tbnz` in `property-load` spilled-slot cold stubs with `±1 MiB` `cmp + b.mi` and reusing `Z` for inherited `csel`, raising `allowColdStubs` for script functions up to `2048` blocks / `8192` IR ops, fixing `rememberTransitionOrValue` `x12` clobber by saving `transition` in `x11` and deferring the remember slow path, ordering `site` load before receiver `generalPair`, and marking inherited `property-load` register-transparent in `packages/codegen/src/aarch64/scalar/temporary-registers.ts`); 2-level chunked `$pc` provenance (`envRec:envField->pcRec:pcField`), shape-based factory detection, RPO ref/alias tracking, own/inherited probe separation (`:o`/`:i`), and dominating `global-cell` forwarding in `packages/ir/src/optimization/property-cache.ts`; per-global-name shared `$gc` prologue caches (`packages/js-engine/src/lowering/index.ts`), terminal conditional direct stores inside inlined methods (`packages/js-engine/src/lowering/inline-methods.ts`), and splitting steady-state general-array push into `scriptArrayPushGeneralFast` (`packages/js-runtime/src/objects/array-append.ts`) so numeric `arr.push(x)` loops inline `scriptArrayPushFast`. Base `1d1256a87`; head `16e57af71`. Apple M5 Max, Node 26.13.1.

On Octane (18 paired rounds, `18/18` ≥75% host idle, `92.9%` base / `93.6%` M4 median idle), **all four programs improve with tight, non-overlapping IQRs**: **`DeltaBlue` improves −22.99%** (`0.996 → 0.769 ms/op`, `12.4×` Node, down from `15.8×`), **`Richards` improves −15.76%** (`0.605 → 0.511 ms/op`, `11.1×` Node, down from `13.4×`), **`RayTrace` improves −15.41%** (`3.346 → 2.843 ms/op`, `5.6×` Node, down from `7.1×`), and **`NavierStokes` edges −0.09%** (`2.720 → 2.718 ms/op`, `1.5×` Node). On the generality suite (18 paired rounds, `18/18` ≥75% idle), **all 18 of 18 kernels improve with IQRs excluding zero and zero regressions**, led by **`numberCall` (−38.75%, `0.9×` Node)**, **`catchInLoop` (−32.01%)**, **`concatenateStrings` (−29.67%)**, **`mathMax` (−29.11%)**, **`builtinMethod` (−27.76%, `0.9×` Node)**, **`objectArray` (−27.72%, `1.7×` Node)**, **`arguments3` (−27.05%)**, **`arguments4` (−24.40%)**, **`mixedCall` (−20.92%, `1.0×` Node)**, **`receivers4` (−20.92%, `2.3×` Node)**, **`receivers2` (−19.23%, `2.3×` Node)**, **`allocateObjects` (−12.09%, `4.8×` Node)**, **`receivers8` (−10.36%, `4.2×` Node)**, and **`objectRoundTrip` (−9.54%, `4.6×` Node)**. On elements/builtins/creation (18 paired rounds, `18/18` ≥75% idle), **11 of 13 kernels improve with IQRs excluding zero and zero significant regressions**: **`mathFloor` (−35.67%)**, **`mathSqrt` (−31.94%, `0.8×` Node)**, **`objectElementRead` (−28.38%, `1.4×` Node)**, **`charCodeAt` (−23.97%, `1.0×` Node)**, and **`pushLength` (−5.49%, `2.4×` Node, recovering M3's regression and beating pre-M3)**. Call and loop kernels (20 paired rounds, `20/20` ≥75% idle) remain well ahead of Node (`0.03×`–`0.4×` Node). `string-bench --suite general` (18 paired rounds, `18/18` ≥75% idle) improves **`upperCase` by −2.32%** (`0.793 → 0.774 ms`, `4.36×` Node) and holds `json` at `−0.51%` (`78.345 → 78.005 ms`, `9.46×` Node). Typed AOT outputs — **frozen v7 `--runtime-tree`** and **`kernels` typed** — produce **bit-for-bit identical** `__TEXT,__text`, `__TEXT,__eh_frame`, `__TEXT,__unwind_info`, and `__DATA,__data` Mach-O sections versus base `1d1256a87`. Both Darwin arm64 `stage1 == stage2` and native `stage1 --target linux-x86_64` cross-emission reproduce their stage-1 images byte for byte under LLDB.

# Untyped Octane M5 · 8-entry inherited IC, scalar array literals, dirty-flag latch, specialized `general-equal`, and indirect site provenance · Darwin arm64

Milestone 5 of the untyped Octane optimization stream on top of M4 (`b73d3fa70`): expanding the inherited/method inline property cache to 8 entries (`propertyInheritedCacheEntries = 8`, preserving cache-line-0 own/store layout across `packages/js-runtime/src/objects/{operations,values}.ts`, `packages/ir/src/scalar/index.ts`, `packages/ts-native/src/closure/lowering/index.ts`, and `packages/js-engine/src/lowering/index.ts`); iterative-worklist scalar replacement of non-escaping fresh dense array literals (`const a = [e0..ek]`, `1 <= k <= 4`) with lazy materialization on non-number/OOB/object keys and structure-tagged deterministic `slotTable` field names (`slotTableTag`) in `packages/js-engine/src/lowering/index.ts`; transactional tiny-method inlining up to `maxTinyCandidatesPerSite = 8` (`nodeCount <= 24`) with direct bail-free terminal stores (`packages/js-engine/src/lowering/inline-methods.ts`); single-boolean `dirtySlot` loop-latch refresh for loops with `> 12` cold invalidation sites and indirect `createScriptPropertyCache` factory provenance through immutable module-export/closure-env records (`analyzeModulePropertySites` under `closedRecords = false` / dynamic-eval hosts) in `packages/ir/src/optimization/property-cache.ts`; script-entry `general-equal` constant/tag folding (`packages/ir/src/optimization/forward-loads.ts`); specialized AArch64 `emitSpecializedGeneralEqual`, inline tag-first write barriers in `emitPolymorphicPropertyStore`, `knownGeneralEqualTags` (gated on `hasScriptOps` to keep typed AOT bit-identical), fixed `general-is` branch-flag fusion for `fn.scriptEntry`, and raised script `allowColdStubs` thresholds (`3584` blocks / `16384` IR ops) in `packages/codegen/src/{scalar/property-fusion.ts,aarch64/scalar/{general-elements.ts,index.ts}}`; and compact `object.internalShape === realm.arrayChildShape` fast guard in `scriptArrayPushFast` (`packages/js-runtime/src/objects/array-append.ts`). Base `b73d3fa70`; head `9450aa5e2`. Apple M5 Max, Node 26.13.1.

On Octane (18 paired rounds, `18/18` ≥75% host idle, `89.2%` base / `89.4%` M5 median idle), **all three OOP/allocation-heavy programs improve with tight, non-overlapping IQRs and zero regressions**: **`DeltaBlue` improves −3.02%** (`0.762 → 0.739 ms/op`, `11.7×` Node, down from `12.4×`), **`RayTrace` improves −1.90%** (`2.831 → 2.782 ms/op`, `5.5×` Node, down from `5.6×`), **`Richards` improves −1.32%** (`0.509 → 0.502 ms/op`, `11.1×` Node), and **`NavierStokes` holds flat at −0.05%** (`2.724 → 2.724 ms/op`, `1.5×` Node). On the generality suite (18 paired rounds, `18/18` ≥75% idle), **10 of 18 kernels improve with IQRs excluding zero**, led by **`receivers8` (−74.16%, `1.08×` Node, down from `4.2×`)**, **`receivers2` (−61.42%, `0.87×` Node, faster than Node)**, **`receivers4` (−56.84%, `0.96×` Node, faster than Node)**, **`catchInLoop` (−24.06%, `0.86×` Node, faster than Node)**, **`objectRoundTrip` (−16.43%, `3.96×` Node, down from `4.6×`)**, **`mixedCall` (−2.81%, `0.99×` Node)**, **`arguments6` (−2.75%)**, and **`arguments5` (−2.73%)**, with one minor `+0.050 ns` (`+1.93%`) drift on `arguments3`. On elements/builtins/creation (18 paired rounds, `18/18` ≥75% idle), **5 kernels improve sharply**, led by **`objectArrayLiteral` (−97.71%, `0.18×` Node, down from `7.6×`)**, **`numberArrayLiteral` (−92.39%, `0.29×` Node, down from `3.9×`)**, **`pushLength` (−14.76%, `2.04×` Node, down from `2.4×`)**, **`numberElementWrite` (−2.73%, `0.41×` Node)**, and **`setAddHas` (−2.24%, `0.76×` Node)**, with minor `+0.05 ns` shifts on `charCodeAt` (`+2.29%`) and `mathFloor` (`+2.14%`). Call and loop kernels (20 paired rounds, `20/20` ≥75% idle) improve **`method` by −1.83%** (`0.16×` Node, recovering M4's `+2.10%` drift) and keep all eight kernels at `0.03×`–`0.40×` Node. `string-bench --suite general` (18 paired rounds, `14/18` ≥75% idle, `81.4%` / `80.3%` median idle) holds steady (`upperCase` `−0.86%`, `4.17×` Node; `json` `+0.45%`, `9.47×` Node, both IQRs crossing zero). Typed AOT outputs — **frozen v7 `--runtime-tree`** and **`kernels` typed** — produce **bit-for-bit identical** `__TEXT,__text`, `__TEXT,__eh_frame`, `__TEXT,__unwind_info`, and `__DATA,__data` Mach-O sections versus base `b73d3fa70`. Both Darwin arm64 `stage1 == stage2` and native `stage1 --target linux-x86_64` cross-emission reproduce their stage-1 images byte for byte under LLDB.
